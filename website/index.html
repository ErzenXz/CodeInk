Installation & release notes

Initial builds may be unsigned. On macOS, use Privacy & Security to approve the app after checking its source. Windows may show a SmartScreen notice. SHA-256 checksums are included with each release.

Early Access installs as a separate app and uses separate session data. Both channels need an independently installed, authenticated agent. Some upstream features, including cloud sharing and session forking, are not available across all agents yet.

CodeInk keeps its own session data on your computer. Your selected agent controls its connections to model providers. CodeInk does not add its own account or collect your CLI credentials.
